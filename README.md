# **Nyay Saarthi** ⚖️  
**Nyay Saarthi** is a **legal aid platform** designed to bridge the gap between **litigants and lawyers**, providing a seamless way for users to seek legal assistance. The platform offers **secure user authentication, admin privileges, and an intuitive interface** to facilitate efficient communication between clients and legal professionals.  

## **Features**  
✅ **User Authentication** – Secure login and registration for users and lawyers.  
✅ **Lawyer-Litigant Interaction** – Enables easy communication between users and legal experts.  
✅ **Admin Privileges** – Admins can manage users, lawyers, and platform activities.  
✅ **Real-Time Updates** – Ensures smooth and up-to-date legal interactions.

- **Backend:** Node.js & Express.js 🌐
